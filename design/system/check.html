Check of the design system

Draws every component for iOS and Android, in the three themes and both languages, and again at 130 % and 200 % of the text size, and measures what a person scanning hundreds of drawings would miss: a control smaller than its platform’s target, a control without a name for a screen reader, a line that is cut, a pane that leaves its screen, and on a whole screen an action that lies out of reach of a thumb. It does not judge how a component looks: that is done by eye, on the system’s page.
