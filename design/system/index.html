Design system

One system of tokens and components from which every screen of Promenade is made: the same idea on each platform, and native to each. The timetable speaks in a sans and in numerals, on glass over the map. The guide speaks in a serif, on a solid page.

    Work package D-02 of the work plan. The Outing is the reference Outing of scenario SC-1, measured on OpenStreetMap data: its names, times and distances are real. The text shown as a Story is a passage quoted from Wikipedia and cited where it is shown. Photographs are from Wikimedia Commons, each with its author and licence.
