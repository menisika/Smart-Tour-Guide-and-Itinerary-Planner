Check of the screens

Draws every screen of the index in every state, for each system it is made for, in the three themes and both languages, and measures what a person scanning hundreds of drawings would miss: a control smaller than its platform’s target, a control without a name for a screen reader, a control cut by its pane or covered by another, a line of text that is cut, a pane that leaves its screen. It does not judge how a screen looks: that is done by eye.
